Hotel Overview

The hotel provides room and bed only, but has a restaurant for food and drinks, the cost of which is added to the guest's bill. This is paid in full upon departure. All rooms in the hotel fall into one of two standards - basic or luxury - and can sleep up to four people. The beds in each room are either single or double beds, or a mixture of both. All of the rooms have no difference as far as "views" are concerned. Check in time is from 2pm onwards. Check out time is from 10am onwards.

All tables in the restaurant are the same and can seat up to four people. Groups needing more seating can simply book more than one table, allowing them to be pushed together to provide a table capable of seating more than four if required.

These assumptions needed to be made to aid in the design process to represent the customer's requirements in a real world situation.
