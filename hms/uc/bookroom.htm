Use Case : Book A Room

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Customer approaches the reception desk and requests a {basic/luxury} room for {1/2} people for {x} nights. Customer also states any special requirements (i.e. Disabled access).    
2 Receptionist enters details of the customer's request. 3 Check for an available room meeting the required specification.
4 Receptionist enters the customer's customer number. 5 Check the customer number is a valid customer number.
6 Receptionist takes a deposit (one night's rate). 7 Print out a receipt for the deposit and print out a confirmation of the booking. Log the booking.
8 Receptionist gives the receipt, booking confirmation and the keys (only if the booking starts now) for the room to the guest.    
9 Guest leaves the desk.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (1) If the customer telephones, the requirements are taken over the phone.
  • (3) If no room of the specified type is available, issue a room of another type and adjust the price accordingly. If there is no room at all, take a contact number.
  • (5) If the customer number is not valid, or the customer does not have one, initiate REGISTER A NEW CUSTOMER.
  • (8) If the customer telephones, send the receipt of payment and the booking confirmation through the post.
  • (9) Guest hangs up the telephone.
