Use Case : Guest Queries Current Total Of His/Her Bill

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest approaches the reception desk/restaurant desk and queries the current total of their bill.    
2 Receptionist/restaurant employee enters the guest's customer number 3 Check the customer number is a valid customer number.
    4 Print out a receipt for the current value of the guest's bill showing the date and time.
5 Receptionist/restaurant employee gives receipt to guest.    
6 Guest leaves the desk.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (3) If the customer number is not a valid customer number, alert the receptionist/restaurant employee.
