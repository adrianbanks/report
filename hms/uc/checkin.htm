Use Case : Guest Checks In

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Customer approaches the reception desk and presents their booking confirmation form.    
2 Receptionist enters the customer's booking number. 3 Check for a valid booking number and that the booking has not already been used. Log that the guest has checked in.
4 Receptionist gives the room keys to the guest.    
5 Guest leaves the reception desk.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (2) If the guest has forgotten their booking number, Receptionist enters the customer's details and gets the booking number from his/her customer record.
  • (3) If the booking number is not a valid booking number, initiate BOOK A ROOM.
