Use Case : Clean Room

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Cleaner cleans a room.    
2 Cleaner enters the room number into the system. 3 Change the status of the room to vacant.

Alternate Cases :

  • (2) If the room needs maintenance, enter the details of the repair into the system.
  • (3) Change the status of the room to unfit. Initiate REPAIR TO ROOM.
