Use Case : Guest Reserves A Table In The Restaurant

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest approaches the reception desk or the restaurant desk and asks to book a table for {1/2/3/4} people at {12/2/4/6/8/10 o'clock} on a particular date. If a table for more than four people is requested, then book more than one table to accommodate the right number of people.    
2 Receptionist/restaurant employee enters details of the customer's request. 3 Check for an available table meeting the required specification.
4 Receptionist/restaurant employee enters the guest's customer number. 5 Check for a valid customer number. Log the reservation.
6 Guest leaves the desk.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (3) If there is not enough room available in the restaurant, alert the receptionist/restaurant employee and exit from the reservation function.
  • (5) If the guest's customer number is not a valid customer number, alert the receptionist/restaurant employee and exit from the reservation function.
