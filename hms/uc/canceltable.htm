Use Case : Guest Cancels A Restaurant Booking

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest approaches the reception desk/restaurant desk and requests to cancel a reservation.    
2 Receptionist/restaurant employee enters the guest's customer number. 3 Check for a valid customer number.
    4 Display the guest's restaurant reservations.
5 Receptionist/restaurant employee chooses the correct reservation. 6 Log that the reservation has been cancelled.
7 Guest leaves the desk.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (3) If the guest's customer number is not a valid customer number, alert the receptionist/restaurant employee.
  • (4) If the guest has no restaurant reservations, inform the receptionist/restaurant employee.
