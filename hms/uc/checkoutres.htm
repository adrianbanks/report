Use Case : Guest Leaves The Restaurant After Eating

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest requests to leave the restaurant after eating.    
2 Restaurant employee enters the guest is ready to leave. 3 Print out a receipt for the items ordered during the meal.
4 Restaurant employee gives the guest the receipt.    
5 Guest leaves.    
6 Restaurant employee enters that the table is now vacant (after the table has been cleared, cleaned and reset). 7 Log that the table is now vacant.
