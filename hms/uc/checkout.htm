Use Case : Guest Checks Out

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest approaches the reception desk and requests to check out.    
2 Receptionist takes the guest's keys and enters the room number. 3 Display the guest's details and billing details. Log that the guest has checked out (change the status of the room to cleaning).
4 Receptionist takes payment. 5 Print out a receipt of payment of the bill.
6 Receptionist gives the guest his/her receipt of payment.    
7 Guest leaves. 8 Initiate CLEAN ROOM.

Alternate Cases :

  • (3) If the details are incorrect, take the customer's customer number and re-check.
  • (4) If the guest queries the bill (i.e. too much), exit the check out function and contact the manager.
