Use Case : Guest Arrives For A Restaurant Booking

 
Actor Action
 
System Response
1 Guest approaches the restaurant desk.    
2 Restaurant employee enters the guest's customer number. 3 Check the customer number is a valid customer number and check that there is a reservation for that customer number.
    4 Display the table number(s) for the reservation. Log that the guest has arrived for the reservation.
5 Restaurant employee informs the guest of their table number(s) and shows him/her to the table.    

Alternate Cases :

  • (3) If the customer number is not a valid customer number, re-check and re-enter the customer number. If the customer number is valid, but there is no reservation, alert the restaurant employee.
